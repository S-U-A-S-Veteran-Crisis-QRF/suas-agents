# PROJECT.md — Where SUAS is and where it's going

_Last updated: 2026-10-24 by Claude (conductor)_

## One paragraph

S.U.A.S. Veteran Crisis QRF ("Shut Up and Serve") is a founder-led California 501(c)(3) in Santa Clara County building a privacy-first veteran support coordination app. Three buttons — Food, Ride, Shelter — each dispatch a real service through a peer bridge. The business model: become a Medi-Cal-reimbursable CalAIM Community Supports provider so every ride, meal, and shelter placement the app dispatches is billable; use grants as bridge capital; keep an earned-revenue floor (janitorial contract, future gov contracts); license the model to other veteran orgs in years 3–5. LLM agents run the back office so the few human hours go to veterans, signatures, and relationships.

## The plan

### Phase 1 — next 90 days: prove the loop
- Get the 25–50 veteran pilot live and documented (consent, incident logs, weekly outcome reports: agents build the paperwork, humans run the service).
- File CalAIM provider enrollment with Santa Clara Family Health Plan. The 12–18 month clock starts at filing, not before.
- Ship the iOS app through the Apple enrollment.
- Close the hardware in-kind lane (phones/laptops via Bay Area veteran ERGs and IT asset disposition).
- Fund it from DOL Stand Down applications and City of San José grants (drafts exist; agents track deadlines).

### Phase 2 — months 4–12: evidence and pipeline
- Turn pilot numbers into stronger grant/contract pitches.
- Continuous funding machine: weekly scans, drafted applications, deadline tracking, one-message outreach to Rotary/ERGs/connectors.
- Advance NIH STTR (via Stanford + a for-profit partner) and the VA Innovation Ecosystem Suicide Prevention BAA concept (non-clinical coordination prototype).
- Reassign the orphaned P0 security items (KMS keys, RLS default-deny, access revocation) — they block real data collection and therefore CalAIM.
- Target by month 12: first CalAIM contract, one government award, gross receipts over $50k.

### Phase 3 — year 2: operate at reimbursement scale
- CalAIM billing live; first paid peer dispatchers (Medi-Cal Peer Support Specialist certified where possible).
- Agents run intake, scheduling, billing submission, denial appeals, compliance reporting.
- Expand transportation to the six highly rural CA counties via the CalVet path.

### Phase 4 — years 3–5: replicate
- Package app + agent playbooks + CalAIM enrollment kit + compliance stack; license to veteran orgs in other Medicaid-waiver states.
